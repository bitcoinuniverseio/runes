How it comes together

Four ideas. One connected experience.

Protocol details matter most when they help you understand what you are about to create, collect, or send.

01 / IDEA

Runestones

Rune messages live in an OP_RETURN output marked for the protocol.

02 / IDEA

Etchings

An etching establishes the rune and its supply personality.

03 / IDEA

Mints

Mint activity follows the terms chosen when the rune was etched.

04 / IDEA

Allocations

Edicts direct rune amounts to transaction outputs.

Keep the meaning clear

Bitcoin confirms the transaction. Compatible services reveal the experience.

Malformed Rune messages can become cenotaphs and burn rune balances. Use a compatible wallet, verify the destination and allocation, and never blind-sign a transaction.
